One sentence, under 200 characters, explaining the modern internet to
someone who has just stepped out of the year 1800 — built only from
things they already know: a post road, a letter, lightning, a library.
`main.py` writes it to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Picture one post road joining every town on Earth: your letter flies
> faster than lightning to any house, and every library men ever
> gathered stands open on your table, free for all to read at once.

The picture: one road for the whole Earth (the network), letters that
outrun lightning (instant messages to anyone), and every library ever
gathered open on your own table (the world's knowledge, free to read,
all at once).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt (197 characters, 1 sentence)
```
